# CS50 SQL Problem Set 4

Welcome to **Problem Set 4** of CS50’s Introduction to Databases with SQL!  
In this set, you’ll build on your knowledge of querying and schema design by creating **views** — virtual tables that simplify complex queries and make datasets more insightful.

---

## Projects Overview

### 1. Census Taker
You are a **census taker** working for the **Nepali government**. After a long journey through the Himalayas, you arrive in a remote village to collect and organize census data.  

Your task is to process and structure the population data stored in `census.db` into **views** that the government can use for official record-keeping and demographic analysis.  

---

### 2. The Private Eye
The **CS50 duck debugger** has gone missing again, and you’re on the trail of a secretive detective.  
In the detective’s study, you uncover a mysterious cipher involving sentences, characters, and message lengths — a **book cipher**.  

Your challenge is to decode these messages using SQL!  
With data stored in tables containing sentences and positions, you’ll use **views** and **string functions** to extract hidden messages based on triplets representing sentence, start index, and message length.

---

### 3. Bed and Breakfast
You’re a **data analyst for the City of Boston**, studying how **AirBnB** has transformed the local lodging scene.  
Using `bnb.db`, your job is to create **views** that uncover insights into property distribution, pricing trends, and neighborhood effects on tourism.

---

## Technologies
- **SQLite** for database management  
- SQL queries for **view creation**, **string parsing**, and **data summarization**
